    How your account is protected

    • Your password is stored only as a salted bcrypt hash.
    • Your session token lives in an HttpOnly cookie that scripts cannot read.
    • The cookie is SameSite=Strict, so other sites can't use it.
    • Sessions expire automatically after 2 hours.
    • Logging out revokes the session on the server immediately.

    Protected content

    This page and the /api/dashboard data behind it can only be reached with a valid, unexpired and un-revoked session. Try opening /dashboard in a private window: you'll be redirected to the login page.
